Designed For Verifiability

Three principles are central to the product design.

Provenance

Trace any mark back to the line that earned it.

Every claim, remark, and point assignment can be traced back to specific sections of the student submission. The interface makes this verification process clear, intuitive, and transparent.

Scope determinism

Grading uses only what you provide.

All context used for grading comes exclusively from user-provided inputs. The system does not rely on hidden or external data sources.

Progressive disclosure

Headline first, detail on demand.

Results are presented in layers. Users can see the most important information at a glance, while detailed supporting information remains available for each submission whenever deeper review is required.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How does it grade subjective answers with no single right answer?
It evaluates against rubrics calibrated on actual student response variations, assessing open-ended reasoning rather than exact string-matching.
Does Assisty replace my judgment as an examiner?
No. Assisty is an assistant, not an autopilot. You retain 100% authority to review, edit feedback, override points, and sign off before grades are released.
How do I know I can trust the marks the tool provided?
Assisty mandates exact text citations for every point awarded (no evidence = zero score).
Is shared confidential data kept secure?
Yes. Data shared are processed only to produce the grades and feedback you request, within your institution’s approved infrastructure, and are not used to train public models.
